How I make campaigns work

Pick creators who fit

Big isn’t always right. I match each creator to the audience and the message. For Main Moorkh Nahi Hoon, food, travel and lifestyle creators each told scam stories their own way.

Start with a real feeling

Every campaign begins with something people already feel: fear of scams, surprise bills at checkout, nerves about dating. OWNLY asked “Why are we paying so much extra?” and creators ran with it.

Do the unexpected

When every ad looks the same, the different one gets remembered. A Yashraj Mukhate jingle for Bharat Connect and creators holding placards in public for Main Moorkh Nahi Hoon did that.

Many ads copy the same moves: a celebrity in a strange place, a surprise collab, a behind-the-scenes wink. People remember the star and forget the brand. I aim for work where they remember the brand.From my deck, “No, you don’t have taste”
